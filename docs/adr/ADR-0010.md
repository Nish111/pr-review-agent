# ADR-0010 · Hand check is the official right-reason measure; the AI judge is secondary

- **Status:** accepted, 24 Oct 2026 (rung 2), confirmed 7 Nov (rung 3)
- **Evidence:** `eval/findings_rung2.md` §3, `eval/findings_rung3.md` §5

## Context
Location-only scoring credits wrong reasons and misses right ones; an LLM judge is cheaper than a person.

## Decision
Nishant hand-checks every location match; Gemini judges independently and is compared; disagreements go to a second round.

## Consequences
Independent judge agreement 6/9 and 18/27, so the judge can't replace the hand check yet. Cost: about an hour of human time per rung.
